## NewGunrizons 1.2.0 for GTNH 2.9

### Vehicles
Fourteen vehicles, one per tier, from a diesel jeep to a reality-bending ark. Every one can be driven, carries
passengers and fights.

| Tier | Vehicle | Moves | Runs on | Weapons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LV | Field Jeep | wheels | diesel | pintle 7.62x39 MG (gunner) |
| MV | Off-road Truck | wheels | diesel | roof 7.62x51 MG (gunner), seats 4 |
| HV | Armoured Personnel Carrier | wheels | diesel / EU | .50 BMG turret (gunner) |
| EV | Main Battle Tank | tracks | diesel / EU | 120 mm explosive cannon, coaxial MG |
| IV | Hover Jeep | hover, crosses water | EU | railgun |
| LuV | Tiltrotor VTOL | flight | EU | chin laser (gunner), rocket pods |
| ZPM | Plasma Fighter | spaceflight | plasma / EU | twin plasma cannons, plasma missile |
| UV | Particle Cruiser | spaceflight | EU + core | particle lance, 2 laser turrets |
| UHV | Antimatter Frigate | spaceflight | EU + core | antimatter cannon, 2 laser batteries |
| UEV | Quantum Ringship | spaceflight | EU + core | phase cannon (through walls), phase turret |
| UIV | Gravity Hover Tank | hover | EU + core | graviton cannon (gravity wells), repulsor |
| UMV | Spacetime Skimmer | spaceflight | EU + core | chrono lance, chrono turret |
| UXV | Dimensional Leviathan | spaceflight | EU + core | rift beam, 3 void laser turrets |
| MAX | Genesis Ark | spaceflight | EU + core | reality eraser, 2 genesis lances |

- Right click a block to park, right click the vehicle to get in. The first one in drives; others take the gunner
  and passenger seats. Shift gets out, sneak + right click the empty vehicle picks it up.
- W/S drive, A/D steer. Flying vehicles climb with Space and sink with C (rebindable: "Vehicle: Descend").
  Spaceflight vehicles go where you look.
- Left mouse fires the main weapon, right mouse the secondary. Each gunner seat aims its own turret; an empty
  gunner seat leaves its gun to the driver.
- Fuel: right click with a cell of diesel (or another combustion fuel) or plasma; electric vehicles charge in a
  Battery Buffer or Charger of their tier. From UV on, the tier's core (Particle Accelerator Segment, Antimatter
  Trap, Quantum Entanglement Core, Graviton Field Emitter, Chrono Stabilizer, Dimensional Rift Emitter, Reality
  Engine) refills the whole battery.
- Rounds load by right clicking the vehicle with them. Plates repair the hull. A destroyed vehicle explodes and
  drops itself with a broken hull.
- Fast ground vehicles run over what stands in front of them. Riders of enclosed seats are hidden in the hull.
- A HUD shows hull, energy, speed, altitude and the weapons of your seat.

### Vehicle parts and the Vehicle Assembly Hangar
- Five parts per tier, 70 in all: chassis, armour plating, engine, control system and drive (wheel set, track set,
  hover emitter array, tiltrotor set or thruster pack). Assembler up to IV; from LuV on Assembly Line, researched
  from the part of the tier below.
- **Vehicle Assembly Hangar** (new multiblock): a 9x5 hall that grows two blocks longer per era. Each bay is roofed
  with that era's Assembly Frames and floored with its Landing Pads; the number of bays is the highest vehicle it
  builds. The vehicle under construction stands in the hall while it works.
- Vehicle quests in the NewGunrizons quest line: the hangar and its bays, the parts of each tier, each vehicle.
