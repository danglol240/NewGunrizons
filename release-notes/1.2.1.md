## NewGunrizons 1.2.1 for GTNH 2.9.0 (stable)

Same content as 1.2.0 (13-tier weapons, 14 vehicles, Munitions Factory, Vehicle Assembly Hangar, quest lines), plus the realistic OBJ vehicle models from the `high-tier-guns` branch, rebuilt for the stable GTNH 2.9.0 release.

### Changes
- Dependencies moved from the 2.9.0-RC-2 manifest to the 2.9.0 manifest: GT5-Unofficial 5.09.54.265, GTNHLib 0.11.54, NotEnoughItems 2.8.171-GTNH, Angelica 2.2.43 (test).
- Vehicles: polygon (OBJ) models with rolling and steering wheels, spinning rotors and glowing parts.

### Install
Drop `newgunrizons-1.2.1.jar` into the `mods` folder of a GTNH 2.9.0 client and server; remove any older newgunrizons jar. Quest lines are in `quests/` of the source tree.
