## NewGunrizons 1.1.0 for GTNH 2.9

### 13-tier weapon progression
Weapons now follow the 13-tier design. Each tier brings a new kind of weapon, not just more damage.

| Tier | Technology | Weapons |
|---|---|---|
| EV | Rail-assisted | XR-7, HX-9 |
| IV | Electromagnetic | Coilgun, Gauss Rifle, Railgun |
| LuV | Laser | Laser Rifle |
| ZPM | Plasma | Plasma Rifle, Plasma Cannon |
| UV | Particle | Particle Beam Rifle, Neutron Rifle |
| UHV | Antimatter | Antimatter Cannon, Exotic Matter Rifle |
| UEV | Quantum | Quantum Phase Rifle |
| UIV | Gravity | Graviton Cannon |
| UMV | Spacetime | Chrono Rifle, Singularity Cannon |
| UXV | Dimensional | Dimensional Cutter |
| MAX | Ultimate | Reality Eraser |

### Firing mechanics
- **Rail weapons** pierce several mobs, losing damage with each one.
- **Lasers** are hitscan: they hit instantly, with no bullet drop. They overheat under sustained fire, and a heat gauge shows above the ammo counter.
- **Plasma** sets targets on fire, knocks them back and partly ignores armor. The cannon explodes.
- **Particle weapons** pierce mobs and ignore most armor.
- **Antimatter** irradiates targets (GregTech radiation; a hazmat suit protects).
- **Quantum Phase Rifle** shots pass through up to 12 blocks.
- **Graviton Cannon** creates a gravity well that pulls mobs in, then crushes them. Sneak while firing to repulse instead.
- **Chrono Rifle** freezes its target and leaves a field that slows everything inside by 90%.
- **Singularity Cannon** opens a singularity for 6 s. It pulls in mobs and loose items and crushes them.
- **Dimensional Cutter** hits everything on a 50 m line, through walls and armor.
- **Reality Eraser** removes the target from the world. Bosses and players take 1000 damage instead. It has a 30 s cooldown.

### Rechargeable energy cells
Energy cells (Laser, Plasma, Particle, Neutron, Exotic, Quantum, Graviton, Time Crystal, Dimensional, Infinity Catalyst) can be recharged two ways:
- put them in a GregTech Battery Buffer or Charger of their tier;
- refill them with charges, like any magazine.

### Munitions Factory
- The factory now grows to 14 segments. Segments 10 to 14 are roofed with UEV, UIV, UMV, UXV and MAX Machine Casings.
- It accepts multi-amp and laser energy hatches.

### Recipes
- New technology modules for UEV to MAX: Quantum Entanglement Core, Graviton Field Emitter, Chrono Stabilizer, Singularity Containment Unit, Dimensional Rift Emitter and Reality Engine.
- With Avaritia installed, these modules are crafted in the Extreme Crafting Table. Without it, the Munitions Factory makes them.
- Weapons, magazines and ammo are still made only by the Munitions Factory.
