## NewGunrizons 1.1.0 for GTNH 2.9

### 13-tier weapon progression
Weapons now follow the 13-tier design. Each tier brings a new kind of weapon, not just more damage.

| Tier | Technology | Weapons |
|---|---|---|
| EV | Rail-assisted | XR-7, HX-9 |
| IV | Electromagnetic | Coilgun, Gauss Rifle, Railgun |
| LuV | Laser | Laser Rifle |
| ZPM | Plasma | Plasma Rifle, Plasma Cannon |
| UV | Particle | Particle Beam Rifle, Neutron Rifle |
| UHV | Antimatter | Antimatter Cannon, Exotic Matter Rifle |
| UEV | Quantum | Quantum Phase Rifle |
| UIV | Gravity | Graviton Cannon |
| UMV | Spacetime | Chrono Rifle, Singularity Cannon |
| UXV | Dimensional | Dimensional Cutter |
| MAX | Ultimate | Reality Eraser |

### Firing mechanics
- **Rail weapons** pierce several mobs, losing damage with each one.
- **Lasers** are hitscan: they hit instantly, with no bullet drop. They overheat under sustained fire, and a heat gauge shows above the ammo counter.
- **Plasma** sets targets on fire, knocks them back and partly ignores armor. The cannon explodes.
- **Particle weapons** pierce mobs and ignore most armor.
- **Antimatter** irradiates targets (GregTech radiation; a hazmat suit protects).
- **Quantum Phase Rifle** shots pass through up to 12 blocks.
- **Graviton Cannon** creates a gravity well that pulls mobs in, then crushes them. Sneak while firing to repulse instead.
- **Chrono Rifle** freezes its target and leaves a field that slows everything inside by 90%.
- **Singularity Cannon** opens a singularity for 6 s. It pulls in mobs and loose items and crushes them.
- **Dimensional Cutter** hits everything on a 50 m line, through walls and armor.
- **Reality Eraser** removes the target from the world. Bosses and players take 1000 damage instead. It has a 30 s cooldown.

### Rechargeable energy cells
Energy cells (Laser, Plasma, Particle, Neutron, Exotic, Quantum, Graviton, Time Crystal, Dimensional, Infinity Catalyst) can be recharged two ways:
- put them in a GregTech Battery Buffer or Charger of their tier;
- refill them with charges, like any magazine.

### Munitions Factory
- The factory now grows to 14 segments. Segments 10 to 14 are roofed with UEV, UIV, UMV, UXV and MAX Machine Casings.
- It accepts multi-amp and laser energy hatches.

### Recipes: Avaritia components, assembled in the factory
- **Weapon chassis** are new components made only in Avaritia's Extreme Crafting Table:

  | Chassis | Main ingredient | Weapons |
  |---|---|---|
  | Crystal Matrix Weapon Chassis | Crystal Matrix ingots | UV weapons |
  | Neutronium Weapon Chassis | Neutronium ingots | UHV weapons |
  | Infinity Weapon Chassis | Infinity ingots and an Infinity Catalyst | UEV and UIV weapons |
  | Spacetime Weapon Chassis | Built from the Infinity chassis | UMV and UXV weapons |
  | Eternal Weapon Chassis | Built from the Spacetime chassis | MAX weapon |

- **UEV to MAX technology modules** are also Extreme Crafting Table only: Quantum Entanglement Core, Graviton Field Emitter, Chrono Stabilizer, Singularity Containment Unit, Dimensional Rift Emitter and Reality Engine.
- The Munitions Factory then assembles these components into weapons. Weapons, magazines and ammo are still made only by the factory.
- Without Avaritia installed, the factory makes the chassis and modules itself, so the mod stays playable.

### Combat drones (LV to MAX)
- 14 drones, from the LV Scout quadcopter to the MAX Sentinel. They have quad, hexa and octo rotor frames (ducted from IV up), spinning rotors, and tilt as they fly.
- **Battery:** flying and energy weapons drain it. Recharge it in a GT Battery Buffer or Charger of the drone's tier.
- **Ammo:** LV to IV drones fire real rounds; sneak + right-click to load them. From LuV up, drones fire from the battery.
- **Combat:** a drone escorts its owner and engages hostile mobs and anything fighting its owner. It never fires through its owner.
- **Returning:** drones fly back and dock into your inventory when their battery or ammo runs low. They land where they are when out of radio range, and can be shot down.
- **New multiblock, Drone Assembly Complex:** a tower on a round platform. Each tier adds a storey to the tower and a landing ring around it (radius 4 + 2 × tier), and the number of storeys decides which drones it can build.

### Structure blocks
- **Assembly Frames** and **Landing Pads**, one of each per tier (LV to MAX), are made in the Assembler from that tier's signature material: Steel, Aluminium, Stainless Steel, Titanium, Tungstensteel, HSS-S, Naquadah Alloy, Neutronium, Cosmic Neutronium, Infinity, Transcendent Metal, Spacetime, Universium and Magmatter.
- Assembly Frames roof the Munitions Factory's segments and build the Drone Assembly Complex's storeys. That tier's GregTech Machine Casing is still accepted in their place.
- Landing Pads form the Drone Assembly Complex's rings. While it works, the drone being built lifts off inside the hangar and a drone of each built tier circles its ring.

### Quest book
- Adds a BetterQuesting quest line, "NewGunrizons: Arms & Drones", in `quests/DefaultQuests`: 70 quests laid out as a grid with one column per tier. Each column runs factory era, then components, then weapon, then Drone Complex storey, then drone.
